# Nine Innings

## Responsive layout is a core principle

Every layout, type, color, and interaction change has to work from a narrow phone through a wide desktop. A layout that only fits one size is unfinished.

- When you have a team in the open season, that team's name and purse sit on the left of the header. The purse is abbreviated, as in 20 BC · 10 REP. The season name is not in the header. The seasons list leaves that spot empty. NineInnings sits level with the team name in the top right, and the coach's name sits level with the purse. The baseball diamond is a logo with no control. It is centered on the sign-in screen at 80 percent of the screen width.
- The diamond scoreboard and the outs counter are not on the page. Each roster kid has a position picker at the bottom of the card, kept to the left. The default is BENCH, in grey. A chosen position is pink. Hovering that closed list turns it black on pink. Positions already held by another kid are grey in the list and can still be chosen, which swaps the two kids. The dashboard puts CURRENT above the next game. Every clock the coach reads is local 12-hour, with AM or PM. No screen uses a 24-hour clock. THE POOL hides when it has no kids. Starting the draft fills that pool, so there is no call-kids form. RECRUITING hides when the board is empty or this coach has no team in the season. SCHEDULE hides while the season is forming. Until the season is active, the dashboard is the setup list through PLAY BALL: the steps already done, the one next, and the ones still ahead. A step this person can do has a button. A coach with no team names one from that list while the season is forming. The next game, the play cards, and played games stay off the dashboard through drafting and through a finished draft. When this coach has a team and kids are on the recruiting board, an active dashboard starts with RECRUITS!. Games start on the dashboard, under the next game. One card plays the neighborhood rabble and waits out the season's practice gap. The other plays a dev game, which does not wait and does not count as the last practice. A later step removes that dev card. The roster does not start a game. The purse gains the small practice bottlecaps. Wins, losses, and reputation stay put. Practice games stay off the schedule. The dashboard lists every game already played, official or practice or dev, and each one opens its report, once this coach has a team in that season. A coach who has not joined does not see that season's games or recruits. With no active season, sign-in stays on the season list. DETAILS ranks the teams by wins, then reputation, once the season is active. That table shows wins, losses, and reputation. Practice and dev games stay out of it. The report is the line, the items that were used, and the writeup. The matchup stays the place to lock a card. The other side is the neighborhood: unlined kids from this roster, then kids who exist only for that game. The clubhouse lists available items on its own tab, and the buy button names the cost. Every item lasts the next game, the next game spends it, official or practice, and a second copy cannot be packed. The shelf says that before the purchase. The metal bat is not a series. DRAFT is a season subtab in the place of SCHEDULE while that draft is in progress, and SCHEDULE is that subtab otherwise. The pool card itself is the pick. DRAFTED shows a count, and expanding it shows the name and the seven stats, with no flavor text. DETAILS starts with CURRENT SEASON. That card titles the season, then lists official days, practice, weeks, draft rounds, and whether you are the commissioner. START THE DRAFT, PLAY BALL, and DELETE SEASON sit on the title line with no border. It says THE DRAFT IS COMPLETE. When the draft is finished, PLAY BALL runs on its own. If it cannot start, the setup row says why, and the commissioner can press PLAY BALL on DETAILS. The confirm asks the commissioner to type the season name. SCHEDULE, THE POOL, RECRUITING, and DETAILS are subtabs on that page, lined up on the left. DRAFT replaces SCHEDULE while the draft is in progress. A menu leaves about one line, then a section header. RECRUITING starts with RECRUITS. RECRUITING shows the kids who are for hire, with the reputation and bottlecap costs stored on each kid. A tryout card is dark blue and marked TRYOUT. There is no claim button yet. The main menu's leftmost tab is DASHBOARD, and that is where the season opens. The coach's next official game in the open season sits at the top of DASHBOARD and names the other team. Within 10 days it lists each weather chance that is at least 10 percent, highest first, and a later game leaves that forecast off. On the schedule, each game sits in a card. Your team is blue. In a matchup, and on the dashboard, your team name stays the same color as the other names. Practice and dev games on the dashboard use a dark blue card. A game within 10 days shows each weather chance that is at least 10 percent, highest first. A later game is listed without that forecast. The schedule opens the matchup. Your nine and packed items sit in one box. LOCK is the action at the top right of the next scheduled game's card. A game after that says LOCK THE NEXT GAME FIRST. The other side sits in a box below, as locked or still open. When the start time passes, that game plays on its own. The matchup then shows the inning scores and each kid's stats in grids, the clubhouse items that were used when any were packed, and a short writeup of the day. Team names in the score grid wrap. That summary leaves off the weather chances. The realized weather stays in the writeup. Every day and clock uses the coach's own timezone. The writeup names each thing that happened, up to four, and closes with two sentences. A game that already has a writeup keeps it. A dev game can be written again while that card is on the dashboard. A win adds that game's bottlecaps and reputation and a win in the standings. A loss adds a loss and leaves the bottlecaps and reputation. A tie leaves the purse and the standings. Screen titles are not painted in green. A bye is named and is not a game. OPEN A SEASON is in the coach menu and opens in the center popup. With a season open, that season's name is the title on DETAILS. It is not in the header and it is not repeated above the subtabs. The first time a coach signs in, the active season opens. The menu is DASHBOARD, SEASON, ROSTER, and CLUBHOUSE. DASHBOARD is the default landing place. The coach's name opens a popup in the center of the screen with the rules version, a line that the list is the season you are viewing and that picking one switches it, the season list with the open season marked, OPEN A SEASON, and SIGN OUT. Choosing a season opens that season. The dashboard shows only that season. Cards share one shape: a title at the top left and action buttons at the top right, on the same line, then a divider and the detail lines, on the field-green background. A picker sits at the bottom left of the card. The active tab in a menu strip uses the pink fill. A button or link turns black on that pink when the pointer can hover. A disabled button or select shows the reason on the page, without hover. A wait cursor is not the reason. Popups use that same card. The row does not scroll. The body under that row holds the rest of the work.
- That body fills the viewport under the header and the tab row and is the only scroll region. The header and the tabs stay put. Do not add a second page scroll.
- Size type and density from the field container. Do not switch the whole layout at one phone breakpoint.
- The header has to fit a narrow phone: the team name, the site name, and the coach name can truncate.
- Touch targets are at least 44px when the pointer is coarse.
- Inputs stay at least 16px so a phone browser does not zoom the page when a field is focused.
- Use `dvh` and safe-area insets.
- Hover styles apply only when the device can hover.
- Field green is `--field`. Fielders are `--player`. Keep the green easy on the eyes, and keep the fielders dark enough to read on the white bases.
